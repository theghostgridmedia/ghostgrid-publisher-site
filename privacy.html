Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

GhostGrid Publisher is a desktop tool that allows users to connect their TikTok account and upload user-selected video content to TikTok for further editing and posting.

Information we access

GhostGrid Publisher may access basic TikTok account information needed to identify the connected account and authorize uploads.

The application may also access authentication tokens issued by TikTok so that authorized requests can be made on behalf of the user.

Video content

GhostGrid Publisher only accesses video files that a user explicitly selects for upload.

Selected video content is transferred to TikTok for the purpose of creating a draft or continuing the posting process within TikTok.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Connect and identify an authorized TikTok account.
  • Upload videos selected by the user.
  • Display basic upload and connection status.

Data sharing

GhostGrid Publisher does not sell personal information.

Information and selected video content may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to provide the upload functionality requested by the user.

Credentials and security

Authentication credentials are not intentionally exposed publicly. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect authorization data used by the application.

Data retention

GhostGrid Publisher is designed to retain only information necessary for authentication, upload processing, and application operation. Users remain responsible for content stored on their own devices and within their TikTok account.

User control

Users choose which videos are uploaded and may revoke TikTok access according to TikTok's account and authorization controls.

Third-party services

TikTok services are governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policies. GhostGrid Publisher is not responsible for TikTok's independent data practices.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as GhostGrid Publisher changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
