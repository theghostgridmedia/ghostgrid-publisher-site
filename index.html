GhostGrid Publisher

A simple desktop publishing tool that helps creators securely connect their TikTok account, choose a video from their computer, and send it to TikTok for further editing and posting.

How it works

1. Connect TikTok Securely authorize your TikTok account through TikTok's official authentication system.
2. Choose a video Select a video from your computer that you want to send to TikTok.
3. Upload to TikTok Send the selected video to TikTok, then continue editing and posting it from TikTok.

Designed to stay simple

GhostGrid Publisher focuses on one task: making it easy for creators to move their own video content from their computer into TikTok. Users remain in control of the videos they select and complete the final posting process through TikTok.

Privacy and security

GhostGrid Publisher only requests the TikTok permissions necessary to connect an account and upload user-selected content. The application does not sell personal information or publicly expose authentication credentials.
